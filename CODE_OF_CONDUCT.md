# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex, sexual orientation, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or caste.

## Enforcement

Contact [conduct@0xshaikh.dev](mailto:conduct@0xshaikh.dev).

We expect:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and personal experiences
- Giving constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, trolling, or inflammatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any sexual advances or sexualised language or imagery
- Threatening behaviour
